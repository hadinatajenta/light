The iPhone Duo hinge crack is the first serious problem for Apple's first foldable iPhone. The $1,999 phone reached buyers on October 23, 2026, and within hours some owners were posting photos of inner screens split down the fold, usually with a bright white band across one side. Apple has acknowledged the issue, says it affects a small number of units, and is replacing affected phones through AppleCare. Two weeks later, nobody outside Apple knows the cause or the failure rate, so this guide keeps confirmed facts and theories apart.

## Quick answer

- Apple says it is aware of "an issue impacting the inner display on a small number of iPhone Duo units" and tells owners to contact AppleCare for a replacement. You don't need AppleCare+ for it.
- The typical failure: a tight spot or crunch while opening, then a crack down the fold and a band of stuck white pixels, often within hours.
- The cause is not confirmed. iFixit's leading hypothesis is a hinge support bar that doesn't drop far enough; Bloomberg's Mark Gurman suspects a bad batch of hinge parts.
- No failure rate exists in public. CEO John Ternus called display returns a "very small percentage."
- Out of warranty, a new inner screen costs $999. With AppleCare+ it costs $99.
- Buying from Apple, with AppleCare+, inside the 14-day return window keeps the risk small. Missing any of those, wait.

## What the iPhone Duo is and what it costs

Apple opened Duo preorders on October 16 and shipped it on October 23, per [iClarified](https://www.iclarified.com/102864/iphone-duo-now-available-to-pre-order-starting-at-1999). US pricing is $1,999 for 256GB, $2,199 for 512GB, $2,599 for 1TB, and $3,199 for 2TB, in Star White or Night Sky.

[Apple's spec sheet](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/) lists a 7.6-inch folding OLED inside, a 5.4-inch OLED outside, and a titanium body that is 5.2mm thick open and 11.3mm closed, at 254 grams. Two lithium-ion batteries share that space; squeezing capacity into bodies this thin is the problem our [silicon-carbon battery explainer](/blog/silicon-carbon-battery-technology-smartphones-explained/) covers.

The inner screen is the part under scrutiny. Apple describes it as "more than ten ultrathin layers and adhesives" topped by a replaceable polymer cover with a matte nano-texture. That texture does a lot of work: [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/News/119895/iphone-duo-teardown) found that without it, the Duo's crease is "arguably even more visible" than the Galaxy Z Fold8's.

## What owners are reporting

The symptoms of the iPhone Duo screen crack match closely from one report to the next. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/25/iphone-duo-displays-cracking/) described a crack down the center line of the hinge, followed by a horizontal block of pixels stuck glowing white. It counted about ten unique cases by October 25, and a couple of owners said theirs cracked during first setup.

Many owners feel resistance first. [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/iphone-duo-owners-report-cracked-screens-apple-foldable/) spoke to Jesse Dalton, whose hinge felt "super tight about halfway up" before "a loud pop" on purchase day, before setup was done. Roneshia Smith told CBS her phone cracked less than 24 hours after purchase, with a sound "like an egg cracking."

As [MacRumors reported on launch day](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/23/iphone-duo-screen-cracks/), an Australian owner had opened his phone fewer than 20 times in about six hours when he felt something inside the hinge, heard a crack, and saw the screen split along the fold. [The Apple Post](https://www.theapplepost.com/2026/10/23/73543/early-iphone-duo-owners-report-screens-cracking-along-the-hinge/) added that he saw no marks or sign of a drop. An Apple Store swapped it on the spot.

[MacRumors says](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/26/iphone-duo-crack-apple-response/) some viral crack photos are AI-generated, so trust the consistent pattern across named owners over any single image.

## How common are these iPhone Duo problems

Apple's October 26 statement to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/apple-responds-to-user-reports-of-broken-iphone-duo-screens.html) says "a small number" and gives no figure. Apple later added that most units are not susceptible and affected ones fail quickly. Independent counts are small too:

- Bloomberg verified more than half a dozen customer accounts but saw no fault on its own review unit or a bought phone, per [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/iphone-duo-crackgate-apple-breaks-silence-on-screen-cracks-offers-replacements-1.500724698).
- AppleInsider asked Genius Bar sources at seven Apple Stores. None had a cracked Duo at first, and a follow-up check found one, as [IBTimes UK relayed](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/iphone-duo-teardown-hinge-screen-cracks-1827710).
- Ternus told Reuters a "very small percentage" of units had come back with display problems, [The Apple Post reported](https://www.theapplepost.com/2026/11/02/73828/apple-cant-predict-when-iphone-duo-supply-will-catch-up-with-demand/) on November 2.
- CNET's folding robot had passed 200,000 folds on its Duo by November 3 with no failure, per [The Apple Post](https://www.theapplepost.com/2026/11/05/73874/you-can-watch-an-iphone-duo-being-folded-thousands-of-times-live/).

Small does not mean fake: in his November 1 newsletter, Gurman pushed back on claims the reports were exaggerated, [MacDailyNews summarized](https://macdailynews.com/2026/11/02/iphone-duo-crackgate-confirmed-as-real-hardware-fault-on-small-number-of-units-amid-severe-supply-shortages/amp/). Our read is a defect in a slice of production, not a phone that breaks for everyone, but without a published rate nobody can give you your odds.

## What causes the iPhone Duo hinge crack

Apple hasn't said. Its [hinge troubleshooting page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148323) states, "There aren't diagnostics or software remedies for hinge issues," so don't wait for an iOS update to fix it. Everything below is a theory.

iFixit mapped reported damage onto a 3D hinge model built from CT scans and patents. The breaks cluster where a gap for a crossing flex cable meets the central hinge support. In iFixit's closing simulation, the center support bar "fails to drop far enough," so the folding screen comes down onto it and creates a stress hotspot. iFixit's Shahram Mokhtari said in the teardown video, [per CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/apple-responds-to-user-reports-of-broken-iphone-duo-screens.html): "Right where the damage is happening, the hinge is shaped a little differently." CEO Kyle Wiens added on X that a "grain of sand can kill the fragile display panel it protects."

Gurman's version is a bad batch: a hinge component that fails to unfold correctly and pushes on the display from underneath. YouTuber CuriousHagi found a thin hinge link sheared where a crack started, but only after folding his unit backward with dirt inside, which IBTimes UK notes doesn't prove a common cause.

Then there's the glass. TechRadar's Lance Ulanoff told CBS that the ultra-thin glass (a sheet of glass thin enough to flex) "could be the most fragile part" of the stack.

## iPhone Duo durability: dust, IP68, and the torture tests

An IP (Ingress Protection) rating has two digits: the first covers dust and other solids, the second covers water. IP68 is the top dust grade plus immersion, and Apple rates the Duo to 6 meters for up to 30 minutes, with a footnote that resistance can decrease with normal wear.

iFixit's point is that a hinge is not what an IP test measures. It worked a Duo open and closed in fluorescent powder (finer than beach sand, similar to office dirt) and wrote, "To be clear, this isn't an IP test." Most powder stopped at adhesive barriers, but "enough reached the hinge to make it crunch." The Duo still collected less visible dust than the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold in earlier iFixit runs, and [MacRumors summed up](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/ifixit-iphone-duo-teardown/) iFixit's view as not a great device for the beach.

JerryRigEverything's Zack Nelson went harder. In [his test, as Dexerto covered it](https://dexerto.com/entertainment/apples-iphone-duo-fails-jerryrigeverythings-durability-test-3421515), hinge dust left the magnets unable to pull the phone shut ("The dusty Duo is damaged"). The inner screen scratched at Mohs level two, against seven for the outer one, and bending left green lines across the inner display until it stopped responding. That's abuse, not daily use, but it points at the same weak spot.

A separate, newer complaint: [Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3271293/new-iphone-duo-hinge-issue-found-by-redditor.html) collected Reddit posts showing a gap between the back panel and hinge on closed phones. Apple hasn't addressed it.

## iPhone Duo AppleCare and warranty: what you'll pay

For the crack, Apple says to contact AppleCare (1-800-APL-CARE in the US and Canada). [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-responds-to-reports-of-iphone-duos-inner-display-cracking/) says you don't need AppleCare+, and carrier buyers should go to AppleCare, not their carrier. Apple has also sent stores a memo telling technicians to inspect the phone and repair or replace it under standard procedures, [MacRumors reported](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/30/apple-alerts-stores-to-iphone-duo-display-cracking/). There is no recall and no public repair program.

Accidental damage is where it gets sticky. Apple's [one-year limited warranty](https://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/products/ios-warranty-document-us.html) excludes "damage caused by accident, abuse, misuse." If a technician concludes your phone was dropped, you're in paid territory, and 9to5Mac reported one buyer who was told the phone must have been dropped and that the repair was on them. [MacRumors listed](https://macrumors.com/2026/10/14/iphone-duo-repair-fees/) the fees:

- Inner display: $999 without AppleCare+, $99 with it
- Other damage: $1,499 without, $99 with
- Cover layer (the top plastic film): $49 without, $19 with
- AppleCare+ itself: $19.99 a month, $199.99 a year, or $399 upfront

Stock is the other bottleneck. CBS found one owner who got a same-day swap and a couple whose replacement was backordered with no delivery date.

## What to do if your Duo cracks

1. Stop folding it.
2. Document it: photos and video of the crack, the hinge, and every outer surface. Apple's hinge checklist includes looking for dents on the enclosure, so clean corners help your case. Note the purchase date and roughly how often you'd opened it.
3. Contact AppleCare or book a store appointment. Ulanoff recommends booking rather than walking in, which also makes sense while replacement stock is thin.
4. Describe it plainly: normal opening, a crunch or pop, then the crack. Mention Apple's acknowledged issue. If you're told it's accidental damage and it wasn't, say so calmly and ask for a second review.
5. If you're inside 14 days, a return is also on the table. MacRumors noted launch buyers had two weeks to return.
6. Skip third-party repair shops: the inner screen repair needs special fixtures, and you'd likely lose Apple's free replacement.

## How to baby the hinge in the first weeks

Apple says affected units fail early, so the first weeks matter most. Open the phone with both hands on the frame in one smooth motion, never by pressing on the middle of the inner screen. If the hinge feels tight or gritty, stop. Mokhtari said many damage reports describe "hearing a crunch, and pushing past it."

Keep it closed in pockets and bags, away from sand, and don't press on it while it's open, which Nelson warned against. Apple's troubleshooting page rules out compressed air. Never peel the inner cover layer yourself. iFixit's summary was blunt: "You'd better baby that screen, Duo owners."

## How it compares with the Galaxy Fold and Pixel Fold

Foldables have stumbled at launch before, as [Fortune pointed out](https://fortune.com/2026/10/27/apple-iphone-duo-foldable-screen-cracking-hinge/). Samsung's $1,980 Galaxy Fold broke for several reviewers within days in April 2019, including Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, now among the reporters on the Duo, per [MacRumors' 2019 report](https://www.macrumors.com/2019/04/17/samsung-broken-galaxy-fold-devices/). Samsung then [delayed the launch](https://www.macrumors.com/2019/04/24/ifixit-teardown-of-samsung-galaxy-fold/) and said it had found "substances" inside some units. Google's $1,800 Pixel Fold failed for Ars Technica's Ron Amadeo after four days in 2023, with no drop involved, [Droid Life noted](https://www.droid-life.com/2023/06/27/reminder-those-1800-foldables-arent-impervious-to-damage/).

## Should I buy the iPhone Duo now or wait

You may not have the choice yet. Ternus said "Duo is constrained as of today," and some configurations ship in January.

If you're getting one anyway, the timing works in your favor: Apple says affected units fail quickly, and the reported cases took hours or a day, well inside the 14-day return window. Buy from Apple, add AppleCare+ (given the $999 alternative), and use the hinge normally in the first two weeks. Ulanoff called the issue "a blip" so far, and the numbers support that, but the foldable iPhone remains a first-generation hinge with a $999 screen.

Wait if you'd buy through a carrier, skip AppleCare+, or can't stand a weeks-long swap, and watch for Apple to name a cause. If what you really want is a bigger screen, our [iPad mini vs iPad Air comparison](/blog/ipad-mini-2026-vs-ipad-air-desk-fit/) is the no-hinge route.

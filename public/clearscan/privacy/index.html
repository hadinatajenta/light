Privacy Policy

Clearscan: Doc Scanner & PDF · Effective October 30, 2026

The short version

  • Clearscan is completely free. There are no purchases, subscriptions or paywalls.
  • Your documents, scans, text recognition results and signatures stay on your device. We never upload them and we have no server that could receive them.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The app is supported by Google AdMob advertising. This is the only reason it connects to the internet.

1. Who we are

Clearscan: Doc Scanner & PDF (“Clearscan”, “the app”) is an Android application published by Pookie Digital Works (“we”, “us”). It scans, edits and converts documents and PDF files. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. You can reach us at contact@hadinata.me.

2. Clearscan is free

Clearscan is free to download and use. Every feature, including scanning, OCR, signing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, locking and unlocking PDFs, is available without payment. We cover the cost of developing the app through advertising, described in section 5. Some actions, such as exporting a document, may offer you the option of watching a short rewarded video ad first; if an ad cannot be shown, you can continue without it.

3. Information that stays on your device

Everything you create or import is processed locally by the app and stored in its private storage on your device:

  • photos captured with the camera and images or PDF files you import;
  • text recognized from your pages (OCR), which runs entirely on the device;
  • signature images and their placement;
  • generated PDF files, tool results, folders and tags;
  • your settings, including an optional app lock PIN, and items in the optional encrypted vault.

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party by the app. It leaves your device only when you choose to share, save or print a file, in which case the file goes to the app or location you select through Android.

4. Information we do not collect

  • We do not operate user accounts or any backend that stores your content.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, files, photos or messages.
  • We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • We do not sell personal information.

5. Advertising and consent

Clearscan displays ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK connects to Google and may process the following, as described in Google’s advertising policies:

  • your device’s advertising identifier;
  • IP address, approximate region derived from it, and device and app information such as model, operating system version and app version;
  • ad interaction and performance data, such as impressions and clicks.

This information is handled by Google, not by us. We never give Google access to your documents.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Without consent, no ads are requested.
  • Privacy options. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options when it is available for your region.
  • Device controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Offline. The app works fully offline. When the device has no connection, no ads are requested.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform for ads and consent. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google ML Kit for text recognition. Recognition models are bundled in the app and run on the device; your images are not sent for recognition. Google’s libraries may collect limited technical diagnostics as described in the ML Kit data disclosure.

These providers act under their own privacy policies.

8. Security

Your documents are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled, so your documents are not copied to Google Drive or another device automatically. You can enable Screenshot block in Settings to prevent screenshots and screen recording in the reader and document views. Files placed in the encrypted vault and PDFs you lock with a password are encrypted.

The app lock protects the app’s interface; it does not encrypt the rest of the app’s private storage. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we recommend also protecting your device with a screen lock.

9. Retention and deletion

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. On your device, you can delete individual documents, empty the trash, or choose Settings → Delete all data to remove the local database and all app-private files. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data. Files you exported or shared elsewhere are outside the app’s control and must be deleted where you saved them.

10. Children

Clearscan is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help you resolve it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over your personal information, including access, correction, deletion and objection. Because we do not collect or store your personal information, we hold nothing to provide, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the controls in section 5 and the tools in your Google Account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will highlight it in the app or in the store listing.

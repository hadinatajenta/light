Light Scripts earns money from affiliate links. This page explains exactly how that works, and what it does not change.

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

## The short version

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you follow one and buy something, we may earn a commission. You pay the same price either way.

## As an Amazon Associate

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That sentence is required by the Amazon Associates programme, and it applies to any page on this site that contains Amazon links.

## Other programmes

We also take part in other retailer and affiliate programmes. Where a page contains links from those programmes, we may earn a commission in the same way. The disclosure at the top of the article applies to all of them.

## What a commission does not buy

A commission never decides what we recommend. Specifically:

- Retailers do not pay for a place in a guide.
- Retailers do not review, approve, or see our content before publication.
- A product is not recommended because it pays a higher commission.
- We do not accept payment in exchange for a positive review.

Where we cannot justify a recommendation, we say so, and some guides deliberately recommend nothing.

## How we choose what to link

We link to a product when it is the thing we would suggest a reader in that situation buy, and when we can point to a reason: a specification that matters, a documented limitation, or a design decision that changes daily use.

We do not invent specifications, prices, ratings, or test results. Where a claim comes from a manufacturer, we describe it as a manufacturer claim. Where it comes from independent testing, we say so. Where it is our own reasoning, we present it as reasoning.

## Prices and availability

Prices and availability change constantly. Anything you see on a retailer's site is the current truth, not what a guide said when it was written.

## How to support the site without paying more

If you find a guide useful and you were going to buy the product anyway, using our link costs you nothing extra and supports the work. If you would rather not, buy directly from the retailer, and the guide is still free to read.

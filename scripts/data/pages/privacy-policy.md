Light Scripts publishes independent buying guides for home-office equipment. This policy explains what data the site handles, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

## Who we are

Light Scripts is an independent publication operated from Indonesia. You can reach us through the contact page for any question about this policy or about data we may hold.

## What we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. The site has no accounts, no comments, and no newsletter.

**Server and security data.** The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address, the pages requested, the referring page, and your browser's user agent. This data is used to deliver the page, to keep the service available, and to protect it from abuse.

**Preferences stored in your browser.** We store a small number of values in your own browser, not on our servers:

- your light or dark theme preference
- whether you have dismissed the cookie notice

These values stay in your browser. They are not sent to us and they are not used for advertising.

**Advertising and measurement.** If we display advertising, the advertising network may set its own cookies and may process data about your visit in order to select and measure ads. Where required, we ask for consent before advertising cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time by clearing the stored consent value in your browser.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not build profiles of individual readers.
- We do not ask for your name, address, or payment details. Purchases happen on the retailer's site, under the retailer's own terms and privacy policy.

## Why we are allowed to process it

Where data protection law applies, we rely on:

- **Legitimate interests** for serving the site, keeping it secure, and understanding aggregate readership.
- **Consent** for advertising cookies where consent is required, which you can withdraw at any time.

## How long we keep it

Technical logs held by our hosting provider are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting, then discarded. Aggregate statistics, which do not identify you, may be kept indefinitely.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold very little, most requests concern data held by our providers.

To make a request, contact us through the contact page. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

## Children

This site is intended for adults making purchasing decisions. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Third parties we rely on

- **Cloudflare** for hosting, content delivery, and security.
- **Advertising networks** if advertising is enabled.

Each of these operates under its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to read the policy of any retailer you buy from.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be described on this page rather than made silently.

This page explains the small number of cookies and browser storage entries the site uses, and how to control them.

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

## The short version

The site works without advertising cookies. We store two values in your own browser to remember your preferences, and we ask for consent before any advertising cookies are set.

## Storage we set ourselves

These are stored in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to our servers, and they do not identify you.

| Name | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme preference | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you clear your browser storage |
| Cookie consent | Remembers that you have answered the cookie notice | Until you clear your browser storage |

If you clear your browser storage, the site will ask again and will fall back to your system theme.

## Cookies set by others

If advertising is enabled on the site, the advertising network may set cookies to select ads and to measure them. Those cookies belong to the network, not to us, and they are only set after you consent where consent is required.

Affiliate links work without cookies on our side. When you click through to a retailer, that retailer and its affiliate network may record the visit to attribute a commission. That happens on their site, under their policy.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary cookies for security and to keep the service available. These are required for the site to function.

## How to control cookies

You can control cookies from your browser:

- Most browsers let you block third-party cookies while still allowing first-party storage.
- You can delete stored data for this site at any time, which resets your theme and consent choices.
- Private or incognito windows discard storage when you close them.

Blocking advertising cookies does not prevent you from reading the site. It only means ads will be less relevant.

## Changes to this policy

If the cookies used by the site change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
